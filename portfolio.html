How do credit hours and the goal work?

Credit hours are the hours of activities that are both Completed and Verified by a faculty reviewer. Planned, In Progress and Unverified activities are not counted. The number at the top of the page is your verified credit hours, and the bar shows how close you are to your goal. The goal starts at 40 hours and you can change it in the box above. It is only a personal target, saved in this browser, and it does not change which activities are verified.
