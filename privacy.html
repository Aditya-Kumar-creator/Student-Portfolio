What we collect

Your name, student ID, email address, and the activities you add (title, category, date, hours, organizer, proof link, skills and notes).

Why we collect it

To keep your participation portfolio, let faculty verify your activities, and produce your activity report.

Who can see it

You can see your own records. Faculty reviewers can see activities that have been submitted for verification. Nobody else can see your portfolio unless you share your exported report.

Where it is stored

In the live version, data is stored in this site's Supabase database and protected by access rules, so a student can only read their own records. In the test version, data stays in your own browser.
